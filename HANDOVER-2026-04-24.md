## Key Decisions Made

1. **Standalone courses** — online courses don't reference the workshop ("As we discussed..."). They work independently for teachers who missed the workshop
2. **No PEP-specific examples in course content** — general learning science with practical classroom examples, not PEP-branded scenarios. Makes content reusable
3. **"Theme" not "Session"** in workshop slides — Rahul preferred this framing
4. **Scenario + discussion merged** in slides — one slide per case instead of separate scenario and discussion slides
5. **Section 5 (Metacognition) rewritten** — shifted from predict-do-compare/assessment focus to plan/monitor/evaluate in daily work (planning a chart, planning the day). More practical and Montessori-aligned
6. **Rosenshine section addresses both programs** — elementary (three-period lesson, materials, work cycles) AND middle school (teacher-led instruction, demonstrations, guided practice). Gaps are shared across both
7. **Mindspark integrated** — referenced in LS 101 Section 4 and Section 9, and throughout the program. Teachers now understand WHY Mindspark works
8. **No grades culture addressed directly** — LS 301 Section 1 is "Why Feedback Matters More Without Grades," tackling the "no grades = no feedback" assumption head-on
9. **Courses practice what they preach** — retrieval starters (Sections 3+ recall earlier sections), interleaved MCQs (one MCQ per section tests a previous section), workshop → course = spacing
10. **Passing score: 80%** for all courses (matching Admissions/PTM courses)

## Important Context

- **Two audiences in one course:** Elementary teachers use Montessori materials-led approach. Middle school teachers use conventional small-group instruction (8-9 students). Content must speak to both — never assume "Montessori materials" when middle school teachers are reading
- **Mindspark** is personalised adaptive software for maths and English, used in both programs. It already implements retrieval, spacing, and adaptive difficulty. Interleaved assignments are possible but not yet set up
- **EI ASSET** is the annual external diagnostic assessment. Referenced in LS 202 as "same principle, daily instead of annually"
- **"Diagnostic question" not "hinge question"** — Rahul's preference throughout all materials
- **Voice exercises default to rank/compare format** — "Three teachers do X. Rank which produces most learning." Not open-ended "explain this concept"
- **Course file format** — markdown with specific syntax: `## Section N:` headers, `### Content` / `### Exercises` markers, `#### Multiple Choice:` / `#### Voice Exercise:` for exercises, `✓` marks correct MCQ answers. Seed scripts parse this format
- **Seed scripts** read `.env.local` from `../../.env.local` (two levels up from `courses/learning-science/`)
- **The workshop is designed for ~20 teachers** in mixed elementary/middle school table groups of 4-5
